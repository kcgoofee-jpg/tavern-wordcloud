# Content & Acceptable Use Policy

**Tavern WordCloud (tavern-wordcloud)** · https://wordcloud.davidzhao.top

Effective date: September 4, 2026 · Last updated: September 5, 2026

> This document is provided in English and Chinese. In case of any discrepancy, the English version prevails. This Policy forms part of the [Terms of Service](#/terms) and sets out what content may be submitted to or produced through the Service, and how related complaints are handled.

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies to all content you upload, process, export, or share through the Service, including chat logs, character cards, world info (lorebooks), regular-expression scripts, feedback snippets you deliberately send, and word-cloud outputs you generate and further distribute.

## 2. Prohibited Content

You must not submit or generate:

1. **Any depiction or sexualisation of minors in any form**, including fictional or AI-generated depictions. The Service maintains a **zero-tolerance** policy in this respect;
2. Content that is unlawful in your jurisdiction, including without limitation the public distribution of obscene material, incitement to violence or terrorism, threats, fraud, and unlawful gambling;
3. Content that infringes another person's copyright, trademark, or other intellectual-property rights, for example by processing and publicly redistributing substantial copyrighted text you are not entitled to use;
4. Content that violates any person's privacy or personality rights, **including uploading another person's private conversations without a lawful basis**;
5. Content containing malicious code, or intended to attack, probe, or disrupt the Service or its users;
6. Content intended to pollute community aggregate statistics, defeat the anonymisation mechanism, or circumvent the Service's technical limits.

## 3. Adult Content

3.1 Consensual adult-oriented source material between adults is a normal use case for this tool, and analysis outputs may therefore contain explicit vocabulary. You must:

- be at least 18 years of age;
- keep outputs containing explicit content away from minors;
- refrain from using the Service to produce or publicly distribute obscene material where prohibited by the law of your jurisdiction.

3.2 The Service's explicit-word tagging is an automated aid; it constitutes no assurance of compliance and does not relieve you of your obligations under the law of your jurisdiction.

## 4. Community Data and Feedback

4.1 Community statistics contributions contain only structured figures — frequent words with counts, message and character counts, per-category word counts, the model name and coarse endpoint class, interface preferences, and word-category corrections — and never chat text or character-card names (see Section 2.4 of the [Privacy Policy](#/privacy)). You must not submit content intended to pollute the aggregate statistics or to defeat their anonymity.

4.2 Cleaning-feedback snippets you expressly confirm may contain fragments of your chat text. Do not submit feedback containing other persons' personal information or unlawful content.

4.3 **Author claims.** By submitting an author claim for a character card, you represent that you are the author of that card or are authorised to act for its author, and that the link you supply is already public and is yours to point at. A claim confers no right: the Operator decides claims at its sole discretion, is under no obligation to decide one or to give reasons, and may reverse an approval at any time. Submitting a false or bad-faith claim is a breach of this Policy. Do not include identity documents, chat logs, or card files in a claim.

## 5. Enforcement and Complaints

5.1 The Operator may, at its sole discretion, refuse to process any content, block IP addresses, or remove feedback and community data, without prior notice and without stating a reason.

5.2 **Child sexual abuse material (CSAM):** suspected instances will be refused outright and may be reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children ([NCMEC CyberTipline](https://report.cybertip.org/)) or a local equivalent as required by applicable law, such as [18 U.S.C. § 2258A](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2258A).

5.3 **Copyright and other rights complaints:** contact the Operator via GitHub Issues (https://github.com/kcgoofee-jpg/tavern-wordcloud/issues), providing sufficient detail to locate the material in question, the basis of your right, and your contact details. For copyright complaints subject to United States law, please follow the notice elements set out in [Section 512 of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/512). Note that the Service does not host User Content; the only retained user-derived data are feedback snippets and anonymised community aggregates, which may be deleted upon a valid complaint.

5.4 The Operator has no obligation to monitor User Content and does not in fact do so proactively. The Operator's handling of complaints is undertaken in good faith and constitutes no determination of ownership or unlawfulness.
